## Bölüm 1 — Ekmek Bitti

**Başlık:** `Saat 8:30'da ekmek biter mi? 😾🥖 #kedihasan`
**Açıklama (ilk satır):** `Hasan'ın ekmek politikası biraz karışık...`

**Sahne 1:**
```
Morning. A young man in a hoodie walks into the shop and asks politely.
Customer says: "Hasan Abi, ekmek var mı?"
Hasan, without looking up from his tea, answers grumpily:
"Bitti evladım. Ekmek dokuzda biter. Otuz yıldır böyle."
```
**Sahne 2:**
```
Close-up. The customer checks his phone, confused, and shows the screen to Hasan.
Customer says: "Abi saat daha sekiz buçuk ama?"
Hasan pauses, sips his tea slowly, and says: "...Bugün erken bitti."
```
**Sahne 3:**
```
Wide shot. Hasan shifts his weight and the camera reveals he is sitting on top of a
pile of warm fresh bread loaves, steam rising. The customer stares at him.
Hasan looks at the camera, unbothered, and says: "Ne var? Sıcak tutuyorum. Hadi kolay gelsin."
```

## Bölüm 2 — Veresiye Defteri

**Başlık:** `Babanın borcu 2019'dan kalma 📒😂 #kedihasan`
**Açıklama:** `Veresiye defteri hiçbir şeyi unutmaz.`

**Sahne 1:**
```
A small boy around 8 years old reaches up to the counter holding a chocolate bar.
Boy says: "Hasan Amca, bunu veresiye yazar mısın? Babam öder."
Hasan raises one eyebrow over his glasses.
```
**Sahne 2:**
```
Hasan slowly opens the huge, dusty "VERESİYE DEFTERİ"; dust flies out. He licks his paw and
flips pages dramatically, then taps a page.
Hasan says: "Baban... Ahmet... Bir ekmek, iki yoğurt. Tarih: Mart 2019."
```
**Sahne 3:**
```
The boy's face falls. Hasan looks at him, sighs, gently pushes the chocolate toward him.
Hasan says softly: "Al bakalım. Ama babana söyle, defter unutmaz. Hadi kolay gelsin."
```

## Bölüm 3 — Telefonla Ödeme

**Başlık:** `Hasan temassız ödemeyle tanışıyor 📱😾 #kedihasan`
**Açıklama:** `Teknoloji Hasan Bakkal'a geldi... sayılır.`

**Sahne 1:**
```
A young woman puts a water bottle on the counter and holds out her phone.
Woman says: "Telefonla ödeyebilir miyim?"
Hasan stares at the phone suspiciously over his glasses: "Telefonla para mı olur kızım?"
```
**Sahne 2:**
```
Hasan takes the phone with both paws and firmly swipes it through the slot of his
old mechanical cash register, then presses buttons on an old calculator. Nothing happens.
Hasan mutters: "Bu çekmiyor. Şebeke yok herhalde."
```
**Sahne 3:**
```
The woman taps her phone on a small card reader hidden under newspapers; it beeps.
Hasan, impressed, then taps his own paw on the reader repeatedly, hoping it will beep.
Hasan says: "Ben de bir deneyeyim... Olmadı. Hadi kolay gelsin."
```

## Bölüm 4 — Bozuk Yok

**Başlık:** `Para üstünü sakızla verdi 🍬😂 #kedihasan`
**Açıklama:** `Her bakkalın değişmez kuralı.`

**Sahne 1:**
```
A man in a suit puts a single chewing gum on the counter and hands over a 200 lira banknote.
Hasan looks at the banknote, then at the man, unimpressed.
Hasan says: "İki yüz mü? Bir sakız için mi?"
```
**Sahne 2:**
```
Hasan opens the cash register: it is completely empty except for one button.
Hasan says: "Bozuk yok. Üstünü sakız vereyim."
He starts counting gum packs onto the counter one by one, the pile growing taller.
```
**Sahne 3:**
```
The man walks out of the shop with his arms full of a huge tower of chewing gum,
struggling to balance it. Hasan waves with his prayer beads.
Hasan says: "Bir daha bozuk getir evladım. Hadi kolay gelsin."
```

## Bölüm 5 — Karşıya Market Açıldı

**Başlık:** `Karşıya zincir market açıldı, Hasan savaş ilan etti 😤🏪 #kedihasan`
**Açıklama:** `Rekabet kızıştı.`

**Sahne 1:**
```
Hasan stands at his shop door, glaring across the street at a huge, shiny, modern
supermarket with bright lights and a long checkout queue. He narrows his eyes.
Hasan says: "Bu dükkân otuz yıldır burada evladım. Kimse yerimizi alamaz."
```
**Sahne 2:**
```
Hasan, with a marker in his paw, writes a cardboard sign and hangs it on his door.
The sign reads: "BİZDE SIRA YOK, MUHABBET VAR".
He nods proudly at his work.
```
**Sahne 3:**
```
Wide shot. The supermarket queue slowly turns around and walks into Hasan's tiny shop,
which becomes packed with people drinking tea and chatting. Hasan sits behind the counter,
satisfied, sipping tea.
Hasan says: "Gördün mü? Hadi kolay gelsin."
```

## Bölüm 6 — Teyzeyle Pazarlık

**Başlık:** `Teyze ile Hasan'ın efsane pazarlığı 🍅😂 #kedihasan`
**Açıklama:** `Sonunda kim kazandı?`

**Sahne 1:**
```
An elderly Turkish auntie with a headscarf and a shopping bag holds up one tomato.
Auntie says: "Hasan, bu domates kaça?"
Hasan: "On lira teyzeciğim."
Auntie, shocked: "On lira mı? Ben seni kedi yavrusuyken bilirim!"
```
**Sahne 2:**
```
Fast-paced back-and-forth bargaining, close-ups alternating between them.
Auntie: "Beş!" Hasan: "Dokuz!" Auntie: "Altı!" Hasan: "Sekiz!"
They both lean forward over the counter, eyes locked.
```
**Sahne 3:**
```
They shake hand and paw, smiling. Then the auntie puts the tomato back on the shelf
and walks out happily without buying anything. Hasan stares after her, speechless.
Hasan says: "...Pazarlık güzeldi ama. Hadi kolay gelsin."
```

## Bölüm 7 — Bir Çay İçmeden Olmaz

**Başlık:** `"Acelem var" dedi, 3 saat kaldı ☕😂 #kedihasan`
**Açıklama:** `Hasan Bakkal'dan çaysız çıkılmaz.`

**Sahne 1:**
```
A stressed young man rushes in, grabs a bottle of water, breathing fast.
Customer says: "Abi çok acelem var, hemen hesap!"
Hasan, calm, pours a fresh glass of Turkish tea and slides it to him.
Hasan says: "Bir çay iç, sonra gidersin."
```
**Sahne 2:**
```
Time-lapse: the clock on the wall spins fast. Empty tea glasses pile up on the counter.
The customer has taken off his jacket and is laughing, telling stories with Hasan.
```
**Sahne 3:**
```
Evening, warm lamp light. The customer suddenly checks his watch and jumps up in panic.
Customer says: "Abi üç saat olmuş!"
Hasan calmly sips his tea and says: "Acele işe şeytan karışır. Hadi kolay gelsin."
```
